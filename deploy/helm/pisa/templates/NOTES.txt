(KEDA must be installed in the cluster):
  kubectl get scaledobject {{ include "pisa.fullname" . }}-worker
  kubectl get deploy {{ include "pisa.fullname" . }}-worker -w

Search runs {{ .Values.search.replicas }} replicas over Qdrant, serving env {{ .Values.search.envId }}.
Load its bundle into Qdrant once (and after each rebuild), e.g. through a port-forward:
  pisa-search load --bundle search/bundle --qdrant http://localhost:6333 --replication 2
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pisa.fullname" . }}-search 8100:{{ .Values.search.service.port }}
